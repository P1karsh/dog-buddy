# Dog Buddy 🐾

A Chrome extension that puts a tiny pixel-art dog on your web pages. Pick a **Golden Retriever**, a **Bernese Mountain Dog** (bigger and fluffier) or a **Labrador** (yellow, black or chocolate), give them a name, and choose what they do:

- **Cheer me on**: sits on your page facing you, blinks, scratches, yawns and tilts their head, and every so often does a trick: spin, hop, play bow, happy pant, bark, head tilt, offering a paw to shake, rolling over, or flopping belly-up. The Lab also has his own trick (below). All tricks are equally likely and each one averages about once every 4 minutes (so a trick every 24–27 seconds).
- **Nap**: curls up asleep on your page with little z's floating up. Click for a sleepy twitch.
- **No-scroll patrol** (works with either): when you open YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok or any site you add, the dog barks, walks over to a big red button and presses it with a paw or their nose to close the tab. You can allow some **grace time** per day first.
- **Auto-sleep**: lies down for a nap when you step away from the computer, then stretches and greets you when you're back.

**Big tricks:** every dog rolls over to the right and flops on his back (then trots back to his spot), and flops belly-up for some lazy paw paddling. The Lab alone does a not-so-scary "grr", then grins and tilts his head.

**Playing with the dog:**
- Click to pet.
- Right-click to shake paws (a little cartoon glove comes in and shakes his paw).
- Rest your mouse still on him for a moment and he holds out his paw; click to take it. He also offers it by himself now and then. Clicking restarts the wait, so petting him never sets it off.
- Drag him around and he walks in the direction you move him. He remembers the spot across tabs.
- Press and hold still to pick him up (legs dangling), then let go to set him down.

The toolbar icon opens the side panel, which shows how many tabs they've booped today. Easter egg: a yellow Lab is called Bruno by default; every other dog starts as Biscuit (your own name choice always sticks).

## Install (developer mode)

1. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`, `brave://extensions`).
2. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and pick this folder.
4. Pin the paw icon to the toolbar and click it to open the side panel.

After editing code, press the reload ↻ button on the extension card, then refresh open tabs.

## Project layout

```
manifest.json          Extension config (Manifest V3)
background.js          Service worker: side panel, sprite delivery, closing booped tabs, boop counter, idle detection
content/dog.js         Runs on every page: the dog's behaviour, drag/pet, tricks, patrol + grace timer
shared/sprites.js      Pixel sprite player (canvas, crisp at any screen scale)
shared/sprite-meta.js  Generated: animation rows, frame timings, anchors
shared/settings.js     Settings defaults, site matching, daily counters
sprites/               Generated sprite sheets: one per breed / coat, plus fx.png
panel/                 Side panel UI
icons/                 Generated toolbar icons (the golden's pixel head)
tools/sprites/         The sprite generator
dev/                   Dev-only pages, not used by the extension
```

## The sprites

The dogs are drawn in the style of the Goldie pack: small pixel art with a 1px dark outline, soft inner lines where parts overlap, a few warm shades and one-pixel eyes. Heads and ears are hand-pixeled per breed and view (`tools/sprites/dogs.mjs`); bodies, legs and tails come from a simple skeleton so every pose and gait stays consistent.

Each sheet has one animation per row (56×44 px cells), in the same layout for every breed: sitting, blinking, happy and tired panting, head tilt, bark, yawn, ear twitch, offering a paw, waiting, the handshake, putting the paw down, the three breed tricks (roll, grin, belly-up), standing (side and front), walking (side / front / back), paw and nose boops, play bow, hop, a spin on the spot through all four directions, sniffing, scratching, lying down, sleeping, waking, being carried and landing. The red button and the little effects (hearts, z's, dust, sparkles) are in `sprites/fx.png`.

To regenerate after changing the art:

```
node tools/sprites/build.mjs
```

Add `--preview <folder>` to also write 3× contact sheets for inspection. Animation timings live in `tools/sprites/anims.mjs`.

## Dev pages

- `dev/sprites.html` plays every animation for each breed and coat. It's generated by the build with everything embedded, so just open it (double-click works).
- `dev/playground.html` runs the real content script and side panel with a fake `chrome` API, so you can test dragging, the Shorts boop, grace time, auto-sleep and the tricks (buttons at the bottom) without installing. This one needs a local server: run `python -m http.server 5178` in this folder and open `http://localhost:5178/dev/playground.html`.

## Notes

- Chrome doesn't let extensions draw on the tab strip or address bar, so the dog lives inside the page. They can't appear on `chrome://` pages, the New Tab page or the Chrome Web Store.
- Short-form detection: YouTube `/shorts/…`, Instagram `/reel/…` and `/reels/…`, all of TikTok, and any custom sites. A custom entry like `facebook.com/reel` only matches that path; `reddit.com` covers the whole site including subdomains.
- Grace time is shared across all patrolled sites, only counts while the tab is visible, and resets at midnight.
